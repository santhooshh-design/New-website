Frequently asked questions

How long does implementation take?

Most teams go live within two to three weeks, including bank connections, approval workflows, and team onboarding. Our implementation team works alongside yours the entire way.

Is Fluxa secure and compliant?

Yes. Fluxa is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data at rest and in transit, and supports SSO, SCIM, and detailed audit logs for every transaction and approval.

Which countries and currencies do you support?

Fluxa supports payouts to 40+ countries in local currency, with more added regularly. Enterprise plans can request custom corridors.

Can Fluxa integrate with our existing ERP?

Fluxa integrates with major ERP and accounting systems out of the box, plus a full REST API and webhooks for custom workflows.
